Our Services

As your local carpenter in Kolding, we provide complete solutions, whether it's repair and maintenance or large-scale renovation projects.

Construction & Extensions

We handle everything from structural carpentry to complex home extensions. We assist with door and window replacement as well as complete roof replacement to future-proof your home.

Custom Solutions & Installation

We create tailored solutions for your home. From custom-designed interiors and fixed installations to all other bespoke carpentry work. We ensure precision and professional furniture assembly tailored to your needs.

Decks & Fencing

We are experts in both deck construction and deck repair. Additionally, we handle the manufacturing and installation of fencing as well as rapid fence repair.

Flooring & Ceiling Renovation

We provide professional floor laying, floor repair, and complete ceiling renovation. We ensure a razor-sharp carpentry finish that lasts for many years.

Kitchen & Bath

We assist you with complete kitchen renovations and the repair of kitchen and bathroom fixtures. We ensure that functionality and aesthetics go hand in hand.

Maintenance & Repair

We offer all types of repair and maintenance for the home, including staircase repair and detail-oriented carpentry for those small but important tasks.
